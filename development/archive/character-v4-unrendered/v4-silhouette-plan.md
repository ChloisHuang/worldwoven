# V4 one-cut contour plan

Schematic: `v4-silhouette-plan.svg`. This is a source design diagram, not a rendered claim.

Keep V3's connected base and accepted-as-progress posterior silhouette. The next cut needs three large connected flows, with a real part and unequal ends. No female or other style expansion, no facial detail work.

1. Main frontal sweep: grows beside a right-side part, passes over the crown and descends toward the left temple. Target peak relief 10–14 mm, tapering into the same scalp shell at root and tip
2. Secondary crown sweep: a little farther back and lower than the main sweep, follows the same general direction without crossing it. Target relief 8–10 mm
3. Short return on the right: visibly shorter, with 4–6 mm relief. Keeps the ear opening clear and balances the asymmetry

The part is a 2–3 mm shallow valley in the continuous shell, around 5–7 mm wide. It must continue to the visible frontal opening. It is not a cutout or a separate dark line floating above the hair.

V3 used five broad overlapping fields compressed to 6 mm. Those fields merged into a nearly uniform raised surface, so the intended locks disappeared in the toon bands. V4 will use fewer separated fields, with enough difference in normal direction to show a few broad ridges and valleys. No extra overlay mesh is planned.

The fringe edge ends at different heights, with the main left sweep lower than the short right return. The ear-to-nape edge should remain gently curved and nonuniform, without a straight cut or repeated pointed teeth.

Acceptance remains actual pixels under the same production toon light and color, followed by gameplay framing later. A triangle-count pass does not establish shape or style acceptance. Preserve V3 images and receipt; save/checkpoint V4 source before the next serial render.
