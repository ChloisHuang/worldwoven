# Isolated anatomical head study

Prototype only, based on verified CC0 MakeHuman hm08 static mesh data. No MPFB addon/code is downloaded or run. `assets/upstream/provenance.json` pins the upstream commit and SHA-256 of the exact base mesh and license texts. Upstream license sections C–D and the mesh's own September 2020 CC0 header agree. Original authoring script crops the body/helper geometry, fits an adult head/neck to Worldwoven's axes/scale, and adds original eye globes and tapered brows.

The visual direction comes only from the supplied sglynp references. Their identity, scars, textures, and images are not copied into the asset or committed. The same brown skin/hair, French outfit, adult masculine-lean body and original round06 side-part framing are retained in the cel comparison.

Run `blender -b --factory-startup --python development/anatomical-head/build_head.py`, then `CLAY_STUDY=1 OUT_DIR=$PWD/qa/anatomical-head/clay01 node development/anatomical-head/render.mjs` and `OUT_DIR=$PWD/qa/anatomical-head/cel01 node development/anatomical-head/render.mjs` under the agreed 175-second/1.2-GiB process-tree guard. Each output directory must be new. Gray clay hides hair/brows to expose anatomy; the cel run uses the unchanged production material ramp and lighting, without the legacy face-lighting-floor override.

Not production-integrated: the imported head/neck currently uses the head classification in the isolated renderer. Blended cervical skinning, existing randomized facial controls, body/hairstyle/pose validation and final neck/collar integration are still required. No all-parameter matrix is claimed. The visual result must be assessed against the reference before any integration proposal.

## Compact adult proportion candidate (2026-10-03)

This second authoring pass is still a separate art study. Source stage `87f8857` records the first actual geometry change before render review; the final candidate is the fitted mesh generated from `build_head.py`. Final local-only images are `qa/anatomical-head/cel09/cel-{front,threequarter,side}.png` and the matching `clay09/clay-{front,threequarter,side}.png`. The two supplied SGLY references and all six final images were personally inspected. No source/reference/render copies were committed, and no production loader, shader, lighting, camera, skin color, hair or outfit code changed.

The whole-face pass shortens the midface moderately, widens the eye aperture primarily toward the temple while protecting the nasal root, preserves the existing alar/nostril and ear surfaces, and softens the cheek into a narrower rounded lower jaw. Upper cervical width is reduced behind the mandible, with the lower neck widening back toward its existing collar fit. Iris rings retain a round frontal projection as the orbital envelope widens. The actual upper-lid contact roll is lowered slightly over the iris and given a small physical roll/recess; the brows now have a lower, flatter turn, a fuller inner body, and a tapered outer tail.

The former light mouth slit was the lower lip's lit return surface. Named source-topology contact vertices now meet the interpolated upper-lip contour, with adjacent lower roll and oral-return support vertices repositioned before Catmull-Clark subdivision. This creates the visible closed lip line in front, three-quarter and profile, including the corners. No teeth, cover plane, painted masking strip or material/ramp changes were used to close it.

**Art status: coherent revised candidate, not an accepted production face.** The mouth closure and slimmer face/neck are visible improvements. The current cel rendering still has a graphic brow shape, restrained visible lid-fold detail and broad cheek tone areas; it does not yet reproduce the reference's refined Sims-style finish. Judge the complete front/three-quarter/profile together. Do not substitute successful rendering or the anatomical base's provenance for the user's art acceptance.

Final scoped checks: Python syntax, unchanged production source, six actual images, no shader/GL errors, and zero retained character references. ## Local orbital, ear-hair and cervical correction (2026-10-03)

- **Orbital opening:** The same-geometry `eye-material09` comparison confirmed that restoring eye reflection alone leaves the narrow white-strip appearance. The full opening was then given more vertical room through its actual skin/globe fit, retaining upper-lid overlap. Globe normals now differentiate the fitted surface, with stable polar treatment; the central polar dot seen during iteration is gone. Bidirectional short-range contact occlusion is traced between the lid skin and globe. The isolated renderer restores the existing view/light-dependent eye reflection channel with `productionRamp:true` and zero skin sheen; no fixed white spot geometry, skin ramp or lighting change is used. `EYE_REFLECTANCE=0` is available only for a same-geometry diagnostic comparison.
- **Ear/hair:** The old underlayer's ear-side edge and old ellipsoid intersected the new cranial side. `fit_hair.mjs` lifts the existing side hair into an arch over the ear, then fits the retained hair against the actual head surface. The prior horizontal cut is absent in the three-quarter/profile images. The ear geometry is untouched. This offline ray fitting is a study workflow, not a proposed runtime cost.
- **Jaw/neck:** The surface was already continuous with smooth normals; the fitted cervical curvature made a real local turn that the cel bands exaggerated. Three masked neighboring-vertex fairing passes smooth only the cervical insertion, preserving the front chin, mouth/nose and ear/collar boundaries. Gray clay is more continuous and the production toon bands still remain, as expected.
