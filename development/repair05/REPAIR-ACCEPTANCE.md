# Door, wall and bath acceptance repair

Baseline: published R16 / Sites v27 / source `60ee26dab923e36925e4c1385d24d96c7f176662`. This is an unfinished repair branch. The unrelated China chairs/archery WIP and character-art prototype remain separate.

## What counts as complete

- Every rigid opening in all four regions, including upper-floor rooms, WC cubicles and stores, has an actual source-wall rough opening and joined jamb/head section. A wall-mounted leaf, displaced surface track or concealing return strip does not satisfy the request.
- Each sliding door has a real hollow wall pocket with sufficient opaque front/back skin and end clearance. Each hinged door has its pivot and complete decorated body coordinated with the true reveal. Meeting joints and running gaps must be constructed rather than hidden by a remote cover board.
- The per-opening inventory identifies its wall plane/depth, rough opening, frame/leaf bounds, floor support and outstanding exceptions. Closed front/back/oblique views and open passage are reviewed for each opening, not inferred from one material style.
- The four Chinese perimeter walls are checked along their complete spans and at all gate jambs and piers. The south entrance has three usable bays; the other sides have real gates. Tests of the center of a gate alone do not prove that the adjoining wall is intact.
- Half-curtains remain fixed hanging fabric. The existing men/women bath noren and offset screens remain without redundant moving entry closures. Full privacy curtains are separately classified and physically suspended.
- Bathing uses a real retaining pool, supported body pose, visible chest-level water and modest temporary clothing. The old pool-interior IDs become poolside bathing approaches; they are not structural walk-through holes in the basin.
- Bath entry and exit work through the normal furniture UI/touch handlers, preserve identity and saved clothing, and produce a usable following camera throughout the transition. Fixed inspection cameras cannot substitute for this product test.

## Validation sequence

1. Lock and remotely preserve each implementation candidate and its test configuration.
2. Prove the door joint and local frame/pocket geometry with preserved known-positive failures. Unknown continuous intervals remain REVIEW, never PASS.
3. Complete the real Japanese wall integration after the pool source is frozen. Then generate the whole four-region opening inventory and resolve every missing mount.
4. Run closed-wall/seam probes against actual scene meshes, whole-perimeter probes, and real two-way controller paths with unchanged actor radius. Cover the new gate bays and existing stairs/furniture routes.
5. Review actual production Three/Mesa front/back/oblique door and bath images. Preserve rejected images, original camera coordinates and source hashes. A rendered file with no GL error is not automatically acceptable art.
6. Run the actual main-controller bath/camera transitions and relevant touch, wardrobe, door hold-open/retreat and region cleanup regressions. Reuse unchanged baseline tests with an explicit hash/scope statement.
7. Only after those gates, integrate into the official Site checkout, build the retained hashed-asset graph, publish to the existing public Site, verify the exact version, and export a frozen source delta for GitHub.

## Current explicit limits

- Mesa/llvmpipe provides actual production shader pixels in the cloud. It is not an iPhone browser test.
- Current source still has known central door leakage until the isolated rebated-joint candidate is integrated. Japanese inset source is prepared but not yet merged. Whole four-region visual/path acceptance is pending.
- Earlier bath candidate02's camera framing obscured the body and is rejected. Its four upper-chest vertex-ratio failures remain recorded; anatomical support/waterline and the actual images govern the corrected design.
- Existing broad character and environment-art concerns remain independent later work. Passing this repair does not mean those designs are finished.
